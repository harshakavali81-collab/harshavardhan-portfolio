06 / Key takeaways

What mattered, and what comes next.

  • KPIs are most useful when users can filter down to the underlying locations and categories.
  • Clear labels help prevent dataset-wide observations from being misread as current market figures.
  • Future improvement: share a verified interactive Power BI publication and check source/refresh availability.
