02 / The problem

The real challenge behind the screen.

Forecast pipelines risk target leakage when future actuals enter lag features, and stock recommendations are often presented without disclosing their assumptions about supplier lead times and service levels.

Project workflow — authored summary
Seeded sales→Past-only features→Baseline vs LightGBM→Forecast horizon→Reorder planner

03 / Process & actions

How the system came together.

01

Build a controlled dataset

Generate 420 days of fictional daily sales for 8 products across 2 stores.

02

Engineer time-safe features

Create past-only lag/rolling windows, calendar features, price and promotion indicators.

03

Benchmark chronologically

Compare LightGBM against last-week seasonal naive using validation WAPE and out-of-time held-out tests.

04

Connect forecast to inventory

Use recursive forecasts, simulated on-hand/order quantities, lead-time assumptions and safety-stock/reorder formulas.

05

Expose the workflow

Serve results in FastAPI and Streamlit, with SQLite request audits, CSV export, tests and Docker Compose.

06 / Key takeaways

What mattered, and what comes next.

  • Date-aware validation is central to forecasting credibility.
  • Inventory recommendations must reveal lead-time and uncertainty assumptions.
  • Future work: rolling-origin multi-step tests, calibrated intervals, real supplier histories and real-world deployment controls.
