The problem

Raw environmental data is hard for everyday users to interpret. PM2.5 measurements arrive at different times; a citywide average hides local variation; weather model fields may be mistaken for monitoring-station values.

My solution

AirAware keeps source provenance visible, reports reporting-station count and missing data, distinguishes historical daily aggregates from forecasts, and presents a map, historical trends and reproducible demo scenarios.

Engineering features

01 · Data ingestion

OpenAQ v3 PM2.5 sensor-day records, weather grid retrieval, UTC parsing, units validation and optional SQLite export.

02 · Prediction evaluation

Time-ordered holdout comparison of lagged Random Forest and persistence baseline, reporting MAE and RMSE only on synthetic examples.

03 · Visual intelligence

React/Recharts charts, Leaflet station view, freshness and coverage scores, demo/live labels and caveated heat-exposure bands.

Honest verification
