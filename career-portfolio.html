01 / DATA / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

Problem. Understand customer behavior and revenue patterns in a raw retail dataset.

My approach. Built Python and SQL data-cleaning routines, revenue and margin KPIs, RFM segments, retention cohorts, dashboards and validation checks.

Outcome and evidence. Reproducible KPI analysis and visual reporting on synthetic transactions; no verified revenue uplift.

What I learned. Analytics is only useful when metrics are clearly defined and data quality is checked.

02 / FORECASTING / OPTIMIZATION

DemandPilot AI — Forecasting & Inventory

Problem. Predict sample product demand and turn forecasts into useful stock recommendations.

My approach. Designed chronological features, evaluated seasonal baselines and LightGBM, then connected forecasts with FastAPI and a Streamlit planning interface.

Outcome and evidence. Demonstrable forecasting and inventory-planning prototype; generated data, not proven retailer savings.

What I learned. Baseline comparisons and time-aware evaluation are central to credible forecasts.

Pythonscikit-learnFastAPIStreamlit

03 / BACKEND / APPLIED MACHINE LEARNING

TransactionShield — Fraud-Risk Scoring

Problem. Make risk-scoring decisions inspectable, with a workflow to review suspicious cases.

My approach. Implemented an educational scoring API, model evaluation, SQL-backed case storage and React investigation views.

Outcome and evidence. Functional prototype using simulated transactions and documented limitations; not certified banking protection.

What I learned. Security boundaries and auditability matter as much as the predictive model.

04 / NLP / INFORMATION RETRIEVAL

EvidenceDesk — Source-Linked Document Q&A

Problem. Help readers locate answers in documents with traceable supporting excerpts.

My approach. Built PDF, DOCX and TXT ingestion and offline TF-IDF retrieval, plus optional semantic retrieval and LLM integrations.

Outcome and evidence. Locally tested source-linked retrieval baseline; optional live generation needs configuration and verification.

What I learned. Source references and explicit uncertainty reduce the risk of unsupported answers.

PythonTF-IDFNLPRetrieval

05 / PUBLIC DATA / TECHNICAL REPORTING

HealthPulse AI — Health Trends

Problem. Present historical population-level health indicators in a reproducible analysis.

My approach. Organized a source-attributed World Bank indicator dataset with Python processing, quality checks, dashboards, FastAPI and React.

Outcome and evidence. The published WDI snapshot reports 1,026 historical aggregate observations; not a clinical service or independently validated predictor.

What I learned. Provenance and careful interpretation are critical for sensitive data.

PythonReactFastAPIData provenance
